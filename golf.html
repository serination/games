Rules
  • Eight cards each, face down in two rows of four. You both start by flipping any two.
  • On your turn, take the top of the stock or the top of the discard pile. A card taken from the discard pile has to go into your grid. A card from the stock can either go in, or be thrown away — and if you throw it away you flip one of your face-down cards instead.
  • Swapping a card in puts the old one face up on the discard pile.
  • Scores: king 0, ace 1, two counts −2, everything else face value, jacks and queens 10.
  • Two cards of the same rank in the same column both count zero — including a pair of twos, so pairing those throws away four points.
  • When someone has all eight face up the other player gets one last turn, then everything is revealed. Lowest total wins.
  • The deck lives in the browser of whoever started the room. Neither of you is shown anything you shouldn't see, but it isn't cheat-proof against someone really trying.
