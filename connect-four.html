How it works

Whoever starts the room plays red and goes first. Games alternate who starts after that.

  • Tap or click anywhere in a column to drop your disc.
  • Four in a row wins — across, up and down, or diagonally.
  • Nothing is stored anywhere. Close the tab and the game is gone.
  • If one of you loses signal, the game ends. Start a fresh room.
