How it works
  • Green means right letter, right place. Yellow means right letter, wrong place.
  • You both get six guesses and play at your own speed. Whoever solves it in fewer guesses wins; same number is a draw.
  • You can see how many guesses they've had and the colours they got, but not their letters.
  • Same word puts the secret in the browser of whoever started the room. It's never shown to them, but it isn't cheat-proof. Set each other's has no such hole, since the word you're hunting only ever exists on the other person's machine.
