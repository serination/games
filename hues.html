How it works and how it scores

480 colours, laid out in rows A to P and columns 1 to 30. One of you gets a colour at random and describes it in your own words; the other hunts for it on the board. You swap roles each round.

  • After each clue the guesser places a guess. Up to three clues, three guesses.
  • The guesser can lock in after any guess. Their best guess is the one that counts, so an early wrong guess costs nothing.
  • Exact square 5 points, one square away 3, two away 2, three or four away 1.
  • Plus one point for every clue not used — nailing it off one clue is worth 7.
  • Only the giver sees the colour, and the guesser isn't told how close they got until the reveal. Clues can be anything: a word, a thing, a memory.
  • Red appears at both ends of the board, since the hues wrap around. That's on purpose.
