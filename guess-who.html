How to play
  • You each get a person from the board. They guess yours, you guess theirs.
  • On your turn, ask one yes-or-no question. They answer, and the turn passes.
  • Knock tiles down as you rule people out. Your board is private to you.
  • Instead of asking, you can guess. Get it right and you win — get it wrong and you lose, so save it for when you're sure.
